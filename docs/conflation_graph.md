# Bipartite Conflation Graph & Component Analysis

## Executive Summary

Milestone 10 transitions the India-Specific POI Conflation pipeline from isolated pairwise comparisons to a unified, topologically grounded **Bipartite Conflation Graph**.

The system models OpenStreetMap (OSM) POIs as the left vertex partition ($V_{\text{osm}}$) and Overture Maps Places as the right vertex partition ($V_{\text{ov}}$). Bipartite edges ($E$) encode cross-source candidate relationships enriched with similarity scores, classification tiers, and contradiction guardrail evidence.

Operating across all **246,856 canonical source POIs** and **605,428 candidate edges**, the graph engine discovers **11,449 active connected components** in **0.14 seconds**, categorizes their structural patterns, detects multi-candidate competition, and strictly prevents transitive over-merging in dense urban corridors.

## 2. Four-Tier Graph Edge Classification

Candidate edges are classified into four distinct graph tiers based on the Milestone 9 explainable scoring engine, distance thresholds, and contradiction guardrails:

| Edge Classification | Universe Count | Percentage | Operational Criteria | Graph Role |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`STRONG_MATCH_CANDIDATE`** | 5,543 | 0.92% | $S \ge 0.75$, `MATCH` decision, distance $\le 50.0\,\text{m}$, zero guardrails triggered | Primary cluster backbone |
| **`POSSIBLE_MATCH`** | 13,390 | 2.21% | $S \in [0.55, 0.75)$, distance $\le 50.0\,\text{m}$, no hard rejection guardrails | Secondary cluster edge |
| **`UNCERTAIN`** | 63,492 | 10.49% | $S \in [0.40, 0.55)$, missing names, generic tokens, or marginal evidence | Preserved for conflict audit |
| **`REJECTED`** | 523,003 | 86.39% | $S < 0.40$ or hard guardrail triggered (`CATEGORY_DISJOINT`, `SAME_BUILDING_DIFFERENT_BUSINESS`, `SAME_BRAND_DIFFERENT_BRANCH`, `EXCESSIVE_SPATIAL_SEPARATION`) | Negative evidence in graph |
| **Total Candidate Edges** | **605,428** | **100.00%** | Full candidate universe generated by Milestone 4 | Complete bipartite graph |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> `REJECTED` edges are never silently discarded. They are retained in the full graph adjacency structure so that downstream clustering and ambiguity analysis can detect contradictions (e.g. if an edge between two cluster members was disproven by a domain guardrail).

## 3. Connected Components & Topological Patterns

### Safeguard Against Transitive Over-Merging
In dense commercial cities like Bengaluru, businesses sit adjacent to one another along streets (e.g., Brigade Road, CMH Road, 100 Feet Road). If connected components were computed across *all* spatial proximity candidates, the entire city would collapse into a single giant component of 50,000 nodes.

To prevent this transitive collapse:
1. **Active Component Discovery:** Connected components are computed strictly across active candidate match edges (`STRONG_MATCH_CANDIDATE` and `POSSIBLE_MATCH`).
2. **Cluster Perimeter Bounding:** Clusters with geographic diameter $> 100\,\text{m}$ or incompatible internal categories are flagged as `AMBIGUOUS_CLUSTER` and quarantined from automatic merging.

### Structural Pattern Distribution (11,449 Total Clusters)
| Pattern | Cluster Count | Share (%) | Description & Conflation Implication |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **`ONE_TO_ONE`** | 8,432 | 73.65% | Clean $1:1$ correspondence (1 OSM, 1 Overture). Prime candidates for canonical equivalence. |
| **`ONE_TO_MANY`** | 907 | 7.92% | $1:N$ star topology (1 OSM linked to multiple Overture candidates). Evaluated for dominant matches vs. duplicates. |
| **`MANY_TO_ONE`** | 337 | 2.94% | $N:1$ star topology (multiple OSM nodes linked to 1 Overture place). Often multiple entrances or duplicate OSM nodes. |
| **`MANY_TO_MANY`** | 115 | 1.00% | $N:M$ complex components. Tightly localized multi-tenant commercial clusters. |
| **`AMBIGUOUS_CLUSTER`** | 1,658 | 14.48% | Clusters exhibiting $> 100\,\text{m}$ geographic spread, category contradictions, or close competing scores ($|\Delta S| \le 0.05$). |

### Cluster Size and Spatial Dynamics
- **Mean Cluster Size:** 2.60 nodes (Min: 2, Max: 144 nodes in massive commercial complexes).
- **Mean Geographic Spread:** 18.71 metres across member centroids.
- **Maximum Geographic Spread:** 284.31 metres (quarantined into `AMBIGUOUS_CLUSTER`).
